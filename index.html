Usage Guide & Tips

Get the best results from your transcriptions with these quick tips.

Selecting the Right Language

For best results, always select a specific language from the dropdown instead of “Auto Detect.” Facebook generates captions in multiple languages automatically, and an explicit language gives the AI a clear target.

Understanding Methods

  • Extract — Pulls existing Facebook captions (instant, 1–3 sec).
  • AI — Downloads audio & transcribes with Whisper AI (30–120 sec, more accurate).
  • Auto — Tries Extract first, falls back to AI if no captions exist.

Wrong Language in Transcript?

If your transcript came back in an unexpected language, select your desired language from the dropdown and try again. This happens when a video has captions in several languages and Auto Detect picks the first one.

Supported URL Formats

Works with facebook.com/watch, /reel/, /share/v/, /videos/, fb.watch short links and Facebook story links. Both mobile and desktop URLs are supported — the video just needs to be public.

About Us

FacebooktoTranscript — Free AI Video to Text Converter

FacebooktoTranscript is a free online tool that converts Facebook videos, reels, live streams and stories into accurate text transcripts. We built it for creators, students, journalists, researchers and accessibility advocates who need the words inside a video — quickly, accurately and without installing any software.

What we do

Paste a public Facebook video link, choose your language and method, and our system either extracts the video's existing captions instantly or transcribes the audio with AI speech recognition. Results can be downloaded as plain text, SRT subtitles or VTT web captions, and long videos can be transcribed in precise 15-minute segments up to 4 hours.

Why it is free

We believe everyone should have access to accurate transcripts. Transcripts make videos searchable, quotable, translatable and accessible to deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers. The tool is free to use with no signup, supported by advertising so it can stay free forever.

Our principles

  • Privacy first: we do not keep your videos or transcripts; processed files are auto-deleted.
  • Accuracy: modern AI speech models tuned for real-world social media audio.
  • Simplicity: one paste, one click — no accounts, no installs, no paywalls.
